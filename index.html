ABOUT UNIVERSITY

A Place To Learn, Grow & Discover

Apex University is a private university located in Jaipur, Rajasthan. Its academic environment covers multiple disciplines and study levels.

The university's academic areas include engineering, computer science and IT, commerce and management, law, pharmacy, sciences, humanities, education, nursing, allied health, agriculture and other professional fields.
